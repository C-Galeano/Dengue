Test design

  • 80% training / 20% test, stratified by diagnosis. The test set is not touched until Phase 5.
  • 5-fold stratified cross-validation on the training set to compare models.
  • Selection metric: macro ROC-AUC (one-vs-rest), the average of "dengue vs rest", "chikungunya vs rest" and "other vs rest". It does not depend on any threshold. Ties within 0.005 are broken by the lower log-loss (better-calibrated probabilities).

Decision rule: the dengue alert

The plain rule "suggest the most likely diagnosis" treats all errors as equal. They are not: sending a dengue patient home as "chikungunya" may mean NSAIDs and no follow-up of warning signs.

Rule
Suggest dengue whenever P(dengue) ≥ {{ info.dengue_threshold }}; otherwise, the most likely diagnosis. The threshold is the highest one that reaches dengue recall ≥ {{ (info.target_dengue_recall * 100)|int }}% on out-of-fold predictions of the training set (never the test set).
