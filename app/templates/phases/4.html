Phase 3 asked "which model is technically best?". This phase asks a different question: does the model meet the success criteria defined in Phase 1, and is it robust enough to be deployed? It uses data the model has never seen, checks that its reasoning makes clinical sense (explainability) and tests it on a newer year and on another country. It ends with a decision: deploy, iterate, or both.
