Searching for data

Key findings

FindingConsequence
Dengue and chikungunya share the same notification form: identical symptoms, findings and comorbidities. Their records can be combined into one dataset.
The Zika form has only 38 administrative columns, with no clinical fields. Zika cannot be learned from symptoms: excluded.
Most cases are closed by clinical-epidemiological criterion, i.e. from the same symptoms. Only laboratory-closed cases are used, so the label does not depend on the inputs.
Brazil's public dengue files contain no discarded cases. "Other febrile illness" comes from chikungunya suspects ruled out by the laboratory.
Numeric lab values (PLAQ_MENOR, lowest platelet count) are empty in 100% of records. Lab information enters as yes/no: leukopenia and tourniquet test.
Severity and warning-sign fields are filled only for dengue cases. Excluded: they would reveal the label (data leakage).
Clear clinical signal: joint pain in 52% of chikungunya vs 16% of dengue; leukopenia twice as frequent in dengue. Symptoms do carry information to separate the diseases.
