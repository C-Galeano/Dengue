A deployed model gets worse when the world changes: patients, disease circulation and data collection all drift over time. CRISP-ML(Q) adds this phase so that the model is watched after deployment, with measurable signals and clear rules for when to retrain. Its results start the next iteration of the cycle.

1. Data drift: Population Stability Index

PSI = Σ (current% − reference%) · ln(current% / reference%). Below {{ m.thresholds.psi_moderate }} stable, up to {{ m.thresholds.psi_significant }} moderate, above it significant.

Symptoms, findings and comorbidities are stable: patients present the same way. What moved is where lab-confirmed cases come from (state of residence) and, moderately, when (month). State of residence is the model's most important feature, so this drift explains most of the drop in performance.
